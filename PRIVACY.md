# ClipGrail privacy policy

ClipGrail is a Chrome extension for saving web pages, selections and links as research sources, organizing them in sessions and preparing them for your own analysis or for an AI chat you choose.

## What ClipGrail handles

ClipGrail handles the following data, only in your browser:

- **Website content:** the readable text of pages you clip, text you select, page titles and addresses, a small picture of the visible part of a clipped page, what the page code says about the page and the trackers it loads, and the email addresses, phone numbers, accounts and payment addresses in the page's links, text and code.
- **Web browsing activity:** the addresses and titles of tabs when you save tabs, and, while you record, the addresses and titles of the pages you open in that window, the page that led to each one and how you reached it.
- **Content you write:** session prompts, presets and notes.

ClipGrail reads the content of a tab only after you act on that tab: a click on the toolbar button, the keyboard shortcut or the right-click menu. When you clip a source saved as a URL only, Chrome first asks you to allow its site; the page then opens in a background tab and is read there. Recording saves page addresses and titles; it does not read page content.

## Where the data is kept

Everything is stored in your Chrome profile on your device, in the extension's own storage. ClipGrail has no account, server, analytics or telemetry, and loads no external fonts or scripts. The developer receives no data from it.

## When data leaves your device

Data leaves your device only when you take it out yourself:

- **Copy Markdown** puts a Research Job on your clipboard.
- **Export** and **Backup** save a file through Chrome's downloads: to your Downloads folder, or where you choose if Chrome asks where to save each file.
- **Open in** copies a Research Job and opens the chat site you chose (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Perplexity). You paste and send it yourself; ClipGrail sends nothing to the site. What you send is then covered by that service's own terms and privacy policy.

You choose what a Research Job includes. Notes, where sources were found, capture times and original addresses are left out unless you include them.

## Sharing and sale

ClipGrail does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose other than the features described here.

## Keeping and deleting data

Data stays until you delete it. You can delete sources, sessions and the deletion log in the extension. Uninstalling ClipGrail removes all of its stored data from Chrome, so export a backup first if you want to keep it.
